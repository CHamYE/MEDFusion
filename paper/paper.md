## 📌 What the paper covers

The survey organises medical image fusion along three axes: the fusion level (data, feature, decision), the clinical task (generation, segmentation, detection, classification), and the evaluation evidence (source-referenced metrics, output statistics, task performance, efficiency, robustness). It reports a corpus of 192 studies, a metric-frequency analysis, and a reproducibility protocol for paired-image fusion.

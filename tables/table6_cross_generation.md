# Table 6 — Cross-Generation Comparison

Methods compared on registration assumptions, pairing requirements, supervision, cost and robustness evidence; method names link to DOIs.

| Method | Registration assumption | Paired-data requirement | Missing modalities | Supervision | Training and inference cost | Robustness evidence and limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [MST-SR (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.inffus.2014.09.004) ; Transform / sparse coding | Pre-registered inputs | Training: natural-image dictionary patches; inference: paired source images | Both inputs required; no missing-input mechanism | Dictionary learning and coefficient rules; no fused targets | Offline dictionary learning; transforms and iterative patch coding at inference | Aligned medical pairs; noise sensitivity and cost depend on decomposition and patch settings |
| [EMFusion (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.inffus.2021.06.001) ; CNN | Corresponding spatial content; no learned alignment module | Corresponding sources for fusion constraints and inference | Both inputs required; no missing-input mechanism | Source saliency and pretrained feature constraints; no fused targets | Pretrained encoder and network training; feed-forward inference | Structural/functional preservation and chrominance enhancement; missing-input tolerance not established |
| [SwinFusion (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1109/jas.2022.105686) ; Transformer | Corresponding grids; attention does not establish registration | Paired sources in training and inference | Both inputs required; no missing-input mechanism | Source structure, texture and intensity losses; no fused targets | Network training; window attention and reconstruction at inference | Multiple fusion tasks tested; arbitrary deformation and missing-input tolerance not established |
| MambaDFuse (2024) ; State space model | Corresponding grids; no explicit alignment module | Paired sources in training and inference | Both inputs required; no missing-input mechanism | Source structure, texture and intensity losses; no fused targets | Network training; selective scans and convolution at inference | Medical pair benchmarks; linear scan scaling does not establish 3D clinical latency |
| [DDcGAN (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1109/tip.2020.2977573) ; Adversarial generation | Spatial correspondence despite unequal resolutions | Training: paired medical patches; inference: both sources | Both inputs required; no missing-input mechanism | Adversarial and source-content losses; no fused targets | Generator and two discriminators in training; generator at inference | Unequal-resolution PET-MRI tested; resolution handling does not establish deformation tolerance |
| [DDFM (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1109/iccv51070.2023.00742) ; Diffusion prior | Corresponding grids for likelihood constraints | No fusion-specific training pairs; both sources condition inference | Both sources required; completion not demonstrated | Pretrained unconditional prior and source likelihood; no fused targets | Pretrained model without fine-tuning; iterative sampling and rectification | Medical pair evaluation; prior mismatch and unsupported generated detail remain concerns |
| M3AE (2023) ; Missing-modality learning | Co-registered MRI volumes | Complete MRI cases for masked reconstruction; available subset at inference | Training: simulated dropout; inference: nonempty subsets | Masked reconstruction, then supervised segmentation and self-distillation | Two training stages; one segmentation model and learned substitute at inference | Missing-sequence tests on BraTS; naturally incomplete training and site shifts need validation |
| [BSAFusion (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v39i5.32499) ; Joint alignment and fusion | Learned stepwise feature alignment | Training: deformed registered pairs; inference: corresponding, unaligned sources | Both modalities required; absence is not the target condition | Modality, alignment and source-fusion constraints; no fused targets | Shared encoder and alignment training; deformation prediction and fusion at inference | Simulated rigid/non-rigid mismatch tested; clinical deformation shifts require validation |
